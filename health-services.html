Quality Healthcare When You Need It

At Family Cure Mission, we believe that every family deserves timely access to quality healthcare. Our health services are designed to provide comprehensive medical care with the convenience and flexibility that busy families need.

From routine check-ups to urgent care, our experienced medical professionals are committed to delivering person-centered healthcare that addresses your unique needs and concerns. We offer same-day and next-day appointments, ensuring you don't have to wait when health concerns arise.

Our Medical Services

Comprehensive healthcare for the whole family

General Consultations

Comprehensive health assessments and diagnosis for a wide range of medical conditions. Our doctors take time to understand your concerns and provide thorough examinations.

  • Physical examinations
  • Health assessments
  • Diagnosis and treatment
  • Medical advice and counseling

Laboratory Services

On-site diagnostic testing with quick turnaround times. Our modern laboratory ensures accurate results to guide your treatment plan.

  • Blood tests
  • Urinalysis
  • HIV testing & counseling
  • Pregnancy tests

Maternal & Child Health

Specialized care for mothers and children, from prenatal care through childhood. We support healthy pregnancies and child development.

  • Prenatal care
  • Postnatal care
  • Child wellness checks
  • Immunizations

Vaccination Services

Comprehensive immunization programs for children and adults. Stay protected against preventable diseases with our vaccination services.

  • Childhood vaccinations
  • Adult immunizations
  • Travel vaccines
  • Vaccination records

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing care and support for chronic conditions. Our team helps you manage your condition and maintain quality of life.

  • Diabetes management
  • Hypertension care
  • Asthma treatment
  • Regular monitoring

Minor Procedures

Treatment of minor injuries and conditions in our clinic. Quick, professional care for wounds, infections, and other minor health issues.

  • Wound care and dressing
  • Minor surgery
  • Suturing
  • Abscess drainage

Prescription Services

Medication prescriptions and pharmaceutical guidance. We ensure you understand your medications and how to take them properly.

  • Medication prescriptions
  • Drug counseling
  • Refills and renewals
  • Medication reviews

Health Screenings

Preventive health screenings to detect conditions early. Regular screenings are key to maintaining long-term health.

  • Cancer screenings
  • Cardiovascular assessments
  • Vision and hearing tests
  • General health checks

Telemedicine

Virtual consultations for non-emergency medical advice. Get professional medical guidance from the comfort of your home.

  • Video consultations
  • Phone consultations
  • Prescription renewal
  • Follow-up appointments
