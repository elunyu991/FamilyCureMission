Meet the People Behind FCM

Our team is composed of experienced, compassionate professionals who are deeply committed to improving the lives of Ugandan families.

From licensed therapists to medical doctors and agricultural experts, each team member brings unique expertise and a shared passion for our mission of creating happier, healthier, and wealthier families. We believe in person-centered care, which means treating every individual with dignity, respect, and genuine concern for their wellbeing.

Our team's diverse backgrounds and specialized training enable us to provide comprehensive, integrated services that address both health and economic needs.
