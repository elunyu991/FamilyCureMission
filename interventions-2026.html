Our Vision for 2026

As we look ahead to 2026, Family Cure Mission is committed to significantly expanding our reach and deepening our impact. Our planned interventions represent a strategic approach to serving more families, entering new communities, and enhancing the quality and accessibility of our services.

These interventions are designed to address the evolving needs of Ugandan families while staying true to our core mission: caring about your health and wealth. Each initiative has been carefully planned to ensure sustainable, measurable impact that creates lasting change.

Key Planned Interventions

Strategic initiatives for greater impact

01

Expansion of Mental Health Services

Dramatically increase access to quality psychotherapy services across Uganda through both physical expansion and technological innovation.

Planned Activities:

  • Establish 3 new counseling centers in Jinja, Mbarara, and Gulu
  • Launch mobile mental health clinics to reach rural communities
  • Expand online therapy platform with multilingual support
  • Train 20 new therapists through our professional development program
  • Introduce group therapy sessions in 10 districts
  • Partner with schools to provide youth counseling services
3,000 Additional clients served annually
15,000 Therapy sessions per year
50% Increase in accessibility
02

Enhanced Healthcare Infrastructure

Build and equip modern healthcare facilities to provide comprehensive medical services to underserved communities.

Planned Activities:

  • Construct 2 new primary healthcare centers in rural areas
  • Upgrade existing clinic with advanced diagnostic equipment
  • Launch mobile health units serving 20 remote villages
  • Establish telemedicine network connecting rural clinics to specialists
  • Implement electronic health records system
  • Expand maternal and child health services
10,000 Patients served monthly
24/7 Emergency care availability
100% Digital health records
03

Agricultural Empowerment Scale-Up

Dramatically expand our economic empowerment programs to lift thousands of families out of poverty through sustainable agriculture.

Planned Activities:

  • Train 2,000 additional families in modern farming techniques
  • Establish 5 demonstration farms across different regions
  • Launch agricultural input subsidy program for 1,000 families
  • Create farmer cooperatives in 15 districts
  • Develop market linkage program connecting farmers to buyers
  • Introduce value addition training (processing, packaging)
2,000 Families trained
150% Average income increase
15 Farmer cooperatives formed
04

Pastoral Family Support Program

Dedicated program to support pastors and their families with comprehensive health and economic services.

Planned Activities:

  • Provide free mental health counseling to 500 pastoral families
  • Offer subsidized healthcare packages for pastors
  • Specialized agricultural training for 800 pastoral families
  • Create pastoral family support groups in each district
  • Provide starter packs for income-generating projects
  • Financial literacy workshops tailored to pastoral families
1,000 Pastoral families supported
80% Achieve financial stability
20 Support groups established
05

Digital Health & Education Platform

Leverage technology to deliver health education and services to families across Uganda.

Planned Activities:

  • Develop mobile app for appointment booking and health tracking
  • Create online health education library with video content
  • Launch SMS-based health tips and reminders service
  • Implement virtual agricultural training modules
  • Establish 24/7 health information hotline
  • Develop e-learning platform for continuous education
50,000 App downloads target
100+ Educational videos
24/7 Support availability
06

Community Health Workers Program

Train and deploy community health workers to provide grassroots health education and basic services.

Planned Activities:

  • Recruit and train 100 community health workers
  • Deploy workers to 50 underserved communities
  • Conduct door-to-door health education campaigns
  • Provide basic health screening and referrals
  • Organize community health awareness events
  • Establish community health committees
100 Trained health workers
20,000 Households reached
50 Communities served
