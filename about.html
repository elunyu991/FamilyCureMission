Our Story

Family Cure Mission was founded with a simple yet profound belief: that every family deserves access to quality healthcare and the means to build a prosperous future.

Born from the recognition that health and economic stability are deeply interconnected, FCM has emerged as a pioneering organization in Uganda, offering an integrated approach to family wellbeing that addresses both immediate healthcare needs and long-term economic empowerment.

Our journey began with a vision to break the cycle of poverty and poor health that affects many Ugandan families, particularly those in pastoral and underserved communities. We recognized that treating illness without addressing economic vulnerability, or promoting economic development without considering mental and physical health, would only provide partial solutions.

Today, Family Cure Mission stands as a beacon of hope, offering three core services that work synergistically to transform lives: professional psychotherapy for mental wellness, accessible health services for physical wellbeing, and comprehensive agricultural training for economic independence.

Our Approach

Integrated care that addresses root causes

01

Person-Centered Care

We recognize that every individual and family is unique. Our services are tailored to your specific needs, circumstances, and goals. You are not a case number � you are a valued member of our community.

02

Holistic Integration

Health and wealth are interconnected. We don't just treat symptoms; we address underlying issues by combining mental health support, physical healthcare, and economic empowerment programs.

03

Accessibility & Flexibility

We offer both in-person and online services with flexible scheduling to ensure that quality care is accessible to all families, regardless of location or circumstances.

04

Evidence-Based Practice

Our methodologies are grounded in proven therapeutic techniques, medical best practices, and successful agricultural models that have demonstrated real-world impact.

05

Community Focus

We prioritize underserved communities, with special programs designed for pastoral families and those who have historically lacked access to quality services.

06

Sustainable Impact

Our goal is not dependency but empowerment. We equip families with skills, knowledge, and resources to build sustainable, independent futures.

Our Journey

Key milestones in our mission

2020

Foundation

Family Cure Mission was established with a vision to integrate health and economic empowerment services for Ugandan families.

2021

Psychotherapy Launch

Launched our professional psychotherapy services, offering both in-person and online confidential therapy sessions.

2022

Health Services Expansion

Expanded to include comprehensive health services with flexible appointments and fast access to treatment.

2023

Economic Empowerment

Initiated agricultural training programs specifically designed for poor families, with focus on pastoral communities.

2024

Continued Growth

Serving over 1,500 families with integrated services, planning major interventions for 2026.
