Our Services

Comprehensive care for your health and wealth

Home / Services

Integrated Care That Transforms Lives

At Family Cure Mission, we believe that true wellbeing encompasses both health and economic stability. Our three core services work together to provide comprehensive support for you and your family.

Each service is delivered with the same commitment to person-centered care, professional excellence, and confidentiality. Whether you're seeking mental health support, medical care, or economic empowerment, our team is here to help you achieve your goals.

Psychotherapy Services

Professional mental health support for individuals and families

Our psychotherapy services provide a safe, confidential space for you to address mental health challenges, process difficult emotions, and develop healthier coping strategies. With both face-to-face and online options, we make professional therapy accessible to all.

What We Offer:

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions tailored to your unique needs and goals

Family Counseling

Strengthen family bonds and improve communication

Couples Therapy

Rebuild trust and intimacy in relationships

Online Sessions

Convenient virtual therapy from anywhere

Child & Adolescent Therapy

Specialized care for young people

Trauma Recovery

Evidence-based treatment for trauma and PTSD

Common Issues We Address:

  • Depression and anxiety
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Grief and loss
  • Stress management
  • Life transitions
  • Self-esteem issues

Health Services

Fast, accessible medical care with flexible appointments

Our health services ensure that you and your family have quick access to quality medical care when you need it most. With flexible scheduling and a patient-centered approach, we make healthcare convenient and accessible.

What We Offer:

General Consultations

Comprehensive health assessments and treatment

Flexible Appointments

Same-day and next-day appointments available

Diagnostic Services

On-site testing and laboratory services

Medication Management

Prescription services and follow-up care

Maternal & Child Health

Prenatal care and pediatric services

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing care for long-term conditions

Services Include:

  • Preventive care and health screenings
  • Treatment of acute and chronic conditions

Economic Empowerment

Agricultural training for sustainable livelihoods

Our economic empowerment program focuses on agricultural training to help poor families, particularly pastors' families, build sustainable income sources. We provide the knowledge, skills, and support needed to create lasting economic independence.

What We Offer:

Agricultural Training

Practical farming techniques and best practices

Business Skills

Financial management and marketing strategies

Sustainable Farming

Environmentally friendly agricultural methods

Cooperative Development

Building farmer groups and networks

Ongoing Support

Mentorship and follow-up assistance

Market Access

Connecting farmers to buyers and markets

Program Components:

  • Crop production and management
  • Livestock rearing and care
  • Soil conservation and water management
  • Post-harvest handling and storage
  • Value addition and processing
  • Record keeping and financial planning
