Our Therapeutic Approaches

Evidence-based methods tailored to your needs

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Identify and change negative thought patterns and behaviors that contribute to emotional distress. CBT is highly effective for anxiety, depression, and many other conditions.

Mindfulness-Based Therapy

Learn to stay present and develop awareness of thoughts and feelings without judgment. Particularly effective for stress reduction and emotional regulation.

Solution-Focused Therapy

Focus on solutions rather than problems, identifying strengths and resources to create positive change quickly and effectively.

Narrative Therapy

Rewrite your life story by separating yourself from problems and discovering your personal strengths and values.

Trauma-Informed Care

Specialized approaches for healing from traumatic experiences, including EMDR and other evidence-based trauma therapies.

What to Expect in Therapy

Your journey to better mental health

1

Initial Contact

Reach out to schedule your first appointment. We'll answer any questions and help you feel comfortable about beginning therapy.

2

First Session

Meet your therapist and discuss your concerns, goals, and history. This is a chance to see if we're a good fit for you.

3

Treatment Plan

Together with your therapist, develop a personalized treatment plan that addresses your specific needs and goals.

4

Regular Sessions

Attend therapy sessions at a frequency that works for you, typically weekly or bi-weekly, to work toward your goals.

5

Progress Review

Regularly assess your progress and adjust your treatment plan as needed to ensure you're getting the most from therapy.

6

Completion

When you've achieved your goals, work with your therapist to plan for maintaining your mental wellness independently.

The duration of therapy varies depending on your individual needs and goals. Some people benefit from short-term therapy (6-12 sessions), while others prefer longer-term support. Your therapist will work with you to determine the best approach for your situation.

Research shows that online therapy is just as effective as in-person therapy for most conditions. Our secure online platform allows you to receive the same quality care from the comfort of your home, making therapy more accessible and convenient.

If you're experiencing persistent sadness, anxiety, relationship problems, or difficulty coping with daily life, therapy can help. You don't need to be in crisis to benefit from therapy – it's also valuable for personal growth and developing better coping strategies.

A good therapeutic relationship is essential for effective therapy. If you don't feel comfortable with your therapist, we encourage you to discuss this with them or request a different therapist. Finding the right fit is important.
